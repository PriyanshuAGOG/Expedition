# World Diabetes Day Expedition 2026 Website Plan

## Experience goal

Create one continuous journey from curiosity to qualified application, then keep the same URL valuable after the expedition as NirogBhumi's public evidence archive. The visual language combines NirogBhumi's nature-led healing identity with a darker expedition editorial system: deep forest, cream, moss, young-leaf green, warm sunlight, documentary photography, fine topographic lines and restrained medical data graphics.

## Core facts carried into the site

- Edition 1 for adults living with Type 2 diabetes.
- Applications open nationwide.
- Approximately 15 participants, subject to medical screening and final clearance.
- A structured programme, not an awareness walk and not simply a trek.
- Journey: Apply → Medical Screening → 60-Day Transformation → 5–7 Day Himalayan Expedition → Field Debrief.
- Destination day: World Diabetes Day, 14 November 2026.
- Simple to moderate Himalayan route, approximately 11,000–13,000 ft only if approved.
- Daily commitment during preparation: approximately one hour each morning.
- No previous trekking experience required.
- Professional mountain team, expedition doctor, daily health monitoring and emergency response protocols.
- The site must not promise diabetes cure, remission, selection or participation.

## Page story

### 1. Hero: the valley opens

Eight landscape photographic layers on desktop and a separately composed five-layer 9:16 scene on phones. The title occupies three depth planes and descends beneath the terrain. Greenery covers more than 40% of mobile screens and more than 50% of desktop screens.

### 2. Expedition briefing: know before you enter

Present a generated overhead Himalayan relief atlas as a documentary field instrument. A fold-out dossier defines the programme's before / on-field / after structure; four physical coordinates explain why it exists, what it includes, who should apply and how medical safety governs it. Desktop notes accumulate like an annotated survey; mobile reveals one note at a time to protect legibility. A generated field compass turns subtly as the atlas advances. This is an orientation instrument, visually distinct from the participant trail.

### 3. Waterfall threshold: enter the expedition world

Move directly out of the hero into a misted emerald ravine. Explain the complete before / on expedition / after expedition arc while the waterfall, mist and transparent foliage preserve physical depth.

### 4. The complete journey

Five-stage physical threshold passage from application to responsible return. On desktop, scrolling drives a cinematic horizontal traverse past five separately generated full-size Himalayan objects: an old kilometre stone, medical-safety cairn, rope-lashed cedar waymarker, high-altitude granite monolith and safe-return stone gateway. On phones, the same markers form a compact vertical switchback. Copy is anchored directly beside the objects without card UI. This is the participant's forward road, not another information map.

### 5. Before the mountain: 60 days

Six programme pillars: medical assessment and monitoring, whole-person nutrition and habits, walking/mobility/strength, sleep and recovery, yoga/meditation/stress regulation, and weekly community accountability.

### 6. The expedition

Present the scale and experience without publishing an unapproved sample itinerary. Show duration, approximate altitude band, group size, destination date, beginner-friendly positioning and professional support.

### 7. Safety is the operating system

Explain screening, physician approval, progressive preparation, final medical clearance, doctor accompaniment, daily checks, guide coordination and emergency response. Medical decisions override summit goals.

### 8. Eligibility and selection

Set expectations before the form: Type 2 diabetes, adult applicant, willingness to commit one hour each morning, availability and willingness to follow medical guidance. Health complexities receive individual medical review; the page does not make automatic clinical eligibility decisions. Selection is multi-stage and not first-come-first-served.

### 9. The evidence trail

The permanent post-expedition archive is built but hidden before departure. It will only be activated when real research observations, methodology, reports, anonymised aggregate outcomes, participant stories, photographs, films and field notes exist.

### 10. Registration

A three-step accessible form:

1. Identity and contact.
2. High-level health and diabetes screening.
3. readiness, motivation, emergency contact and consent.

The landing-page prototype does not transmit or store sensitive health data. Production launch requires an approved secure endpoint, access controls, consent copy, retention policy and privacy review. Medical reports are requested only later through a secure clinical process.

### 11. FAQs and responsible footer

Clarify cost status, selection, experience, medication, medical clearance, daily commitment and data publishing. Include medical and emergency disclaimers and NirogBhumi contact details.

## Interaction system

- Hero: scroll-only depth translation, no animated zoom.
- Soundscape: supplied continuous forest recording, requested immediately on entry with a first-interaction retry for browser autoplay restrictions.
- Story transition: mist plus alternating canopy and moss-terrain seams overlap every neighboring chapter.
- Journey: full-screen horizontal threshold passage on desktop and a compact vertical switchback on mobile, with five generated Himalayan markers and active scroll progress.
- Living UI: all 24 independently generated transparent botanical assets appear as explicit foreground layers—canopies, groundcover, side vines, flowers and component accents; density is reduced on mobile and decoration never receives focus or blocks content.
- Programme: touch-friendly horizontal cards on mobile, grid on desktop.
- Evidence archive: retained but hidden until post-expedition publication.
- Registration: progressive three-step form, inline validation and review state.
- Motion: reduced-motion mode, transform/opacity-only animation and intersection-based activation.
- Sound: request immediate playback of one uninterrupted waterfall-gully field recording with no layered or synthetic elements; browser-restricted playback starts on first interaction, with a separate mute control.
- Performance: WebP imagery, lazy loading below the fold, `content-visibility`, no framework or runtime dependency.
